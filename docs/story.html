Why monaco-json-studio-base-body Exists

Handlebars (.hbs) 1-, 2-, and 3-pane body layouts paired with modular js/monaco/ runtime and source detection.

Design Story

Separating the body workspace from headers and footers lets a single page display 1, 2, or 3 editor panes under one shared header and footer.

In v3, Monaco logic lives cleanly inside assets/v3/js/monaco/ (loader, source, mount, value, events, plus index.js orchestrator). loadInitialSource() detects local-data, same-origin, external URLs, and load failures, reporting metadata via onPaneSource().
