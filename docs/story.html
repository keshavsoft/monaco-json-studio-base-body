Why monaco-json-studio-base-body Exists

Handlebars (.hbs) 1-, 2-, and 3-pane body layouts with the Monaco editor mounting runtime.

Design Story

Separating the body workspace from headers and footers lets a single page display 1, 2, or 3 editor panes under one shared header and footer.

All layout markup lives in single.hbs, double.hbs, triple.hbs, and pane.hbs, while assets/v1/js/monaco.js exposes instance-scoped mountMonacoEditor() helpers so multiple panes never collide.
