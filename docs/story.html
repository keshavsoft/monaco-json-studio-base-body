Why monaco-json-studio-base-body Exists

Handlebars (.hbs) 1-, 2-, and 3-pane body layouts with the Monaco editor mounting runtime.

Design Story

Separating the body workspace from headers and footers lets a single page display 1, 2, or 3 editor panes under one shared header and footer.

All layout markup lives in single.hbs, double.hbs, triple.hbs, and pane.hbs. Starting in v3, Monaco logic lives separately under assets/v3/js/monaco/ (loader, source, mount, value, events) with loadInitialSource() reporting whether data came from local-data, same-origin, external, or failed to load.
